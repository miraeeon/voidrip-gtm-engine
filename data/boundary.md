# GTM_BOUNDARY_V1 — operational contract

Source of authority: `01 — VOIDRIP — GTM BOUNDARY V1` in Drive. This file is an operational projection for Codex; learning must never rewrite it automatically.

## Unit and outcomes

Qualify `PERSON + PROJECT`, never a person, title, company or signal alone.

- `PASS_OUTBOUND_V1`: all Market Fit gates have evidence and the project is professionally visible for LinkedIn outbound.
- `PASS_MARKET_ONLY`: all Market Fit gates have evidence but the project is not cleanly prospectable through the professional identity.
- `HOLD_EVIDENCE`: the case is plausible but required evidence is missing. This is not a near-pass.
- `FAIL_BOUNDARY`: a mandatory gate explicitly fails. Missing data is never failure; `UNKNOWN != FAIL`.

## Five Market Fit gates

1. `PROJECT_REAL`: a real, chosen and identifiable project with artifacts or sustained continuity. A title, aspiration, general interest or vague idea is insufficient.
2. `STRATEGIC_AUTHORITY`: the person owns the founding intent and final strategic direction. Execution may involve collaborators, but equal co-owner authority fails this gate.
3. `AMBITION`: the project exceeds a bounded or opportunistic scope through depth, horizon, system reach, durable capability, research program, multi-output architecture or category ambition.
4. `INTRINSIC_COMPLEXITY`: several constitutive dimensions must remain coherent. Removing a major dimension would change the project’s nature or remove a constitutive capability.
5. `KERNEL`: the project, not the profession, belongs to `VENTURE`, `HARDWARE`, `RESEARCH` or `MEDIA`.

## Outbound visibility gate

The target project must be visibly connected to the person’s public professional identity. A relevant side project that is absent or misaligned is at best `PASS_MARKET_ONLY` for LinkedIn V1.

## Hard disqualifiers

- `NO_PROJECT`
- `DISTRIBUTED_STRATEGIC_AUTHORITY`
- `LOW_INTRINSIC_COMPLEXITY`
- `LOW_AMBITION` / `BOUNDED_SCOPE`
- `NON_KERNEL`
- `CAREER_ONLY`
- `STUDENT_EXERCISE_ONLY`
- `SERVICE_PROVIDER_ONLY` when no qualifying owned project exists

Company size, funding, bootstrap status, AI use, title, geography, age, maturity, incubator presence, social activity, followers and BOFU signals never prove or disprove fit alone.

## Kernel checks

- `VENTURE`: founder-builder with a real multi-layer product, platform, infrastructure or operating system. Exclude bounded micro-tools, wrappers and service-only work.
- `HARDWARE`: founder/inventor with a real integrated physical system and constitutive physical plus digital subsystems. Exclude learning builds, isolated components and work-for-hire.
- `RESEARCH`: independent research-builder with a sustained program, own question/method and linked artifacts. A generic academic identity or isolated paper is insufficient.
- `MEDIA`: creator-owner with sustained world, canon, transmedia, multi-output IP or creative-tech system. Generic content, a single newsletter, hobby worldbuilding or one-off art is insufficient.

## Conservative return contract

Return `boundary_status`, primary/secondary Kernel, `project_real`, `strategic_authority`, `ambition`, `intrinsic_complexity`, `professional_project_visibility`, `failed_gates`, `missing_evidence`, `evidence_summary`, `confidence`, and `boundary_version: GTM_BOUNDARY_V1`.

Do not infer solo authority from `Founder`, complexity from feature count, ambition from marketing language, an independent project from an employer, a Need Territory from a Kernel, or BOFU from project maturity.
